About CounterCrop

CounterCrop is a small English-language guide site about growing microgreens in apartments and other tight indoor spaces. The goal is simple: help beginners get a clean first harvest without needing a garden, a greenhouse, or a social feed full of conflicting advice.

We focus on evergreen how-to topics — trays, seed choice, watering, light, mold prevention, and small-space workflows. Pages are written to be useful on their own. You do not need to join a group, leave a comment, or book a call to use them.

How this site may be supported

In the future, pages may include display ads (for example Google AdSense) and affiliate links to trays, seeds, grow lights, timers, and similar supplies. If a product link earns a commission, it will be disclosed on that page. Recommendations will still prioritize beginner reliability over hype.

Contact

There is no live chat. For privacy or takedown requests, use the email address listed on the Privacy page once the site is live on a custom domain.
