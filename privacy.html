Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai)

CounterCrop (“this site”) publishes informational gardening guides. This policy explains what limited data may be collected when the site is hosted publicly and when advertising or analytics are enabled later.

Information we collect

  • Server logs. Hosting providers (for example Cloudflare Pages or Netlify) may automatically log IP address, user agent, referrer, and request paths for security and reliability.
  • Cookies and ads (when enabled). If Google AdSense or similar ad networks are added, those partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization through your browser settings and Google’s ad settings.
  • Affiliate clicks (when enabled). Product links may pass through affiliate networks (for example Amazon Associates). Those networks may record that a click came from this site in order to attribute commissions.
  • Analytics (optional). A privacy-respecting analytics tool may be added later to count page views. If so, this page will be updated with the tool name.

What we do not do

  • We do not require accounts or collect passwords.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data through forms on these static pages.
  • We do not sell email lists — there is no mailing list on the launch version of this site.

Children

Content is general informational material and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party links

Guides may link to retailers, seed suppliers, or extension resources. Their privacy practices are their own. Review those sites before purchasing or submitting information.

Changes

If monetization tools are added, this policy will be updated with the partner names and cookie categories before those scripts go live.
